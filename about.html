About Gaisen

Gaisen was born from a simple conviction: that luxury and the street are not opposites, but reflections of the same desire — to move through the world with intention.

Founded in Berlin, the house draws from the city's architecture of silence and tension. Each piece is constructed to hold its own, to age with the wearer, to mean something.

We work with European mills exclusively, sourcing fabrics chosen not for trend but for weight, hand, and longevity. Our patterns are developed in-house, refined across multiple fittings before a single piece reaches production.

Gaisen is not fast. It is not loud. It is for those who understand the difference.

01

Material

Every fabric is sourced from European mills with a minimum 80-year history. We touch the material before we design the garment.

02

Construction

Seams are finished to last a decade. Pattern-making is done in Berlin. Adjustments are made until the silhouette is correct — not approximate.

03

Edition

Collections are produced in limited runs. When a piece is gone, it is gone. No restock. No archive sale. This is intentional.
